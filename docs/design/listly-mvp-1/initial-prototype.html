    Assumptions to validate

    What this prototype assumes. Each [H] item is a hypothesis to confirm in the Phase 0 prototype test or with the PM. The design decisions (DD-nn) and open questions (PQ-nn) are in ux-listly-mvp-1-design.md; the wording is in ux-copy.md.

    • [H] Photos from one haul group into items accurately enough that checking beats tagging. Move, Split and Merge fix it; "Next item" while shooting helps (R-5, S-08).
    • [H] Resellers answer by voice in a normal home; typing is the fallback (R-6).
    • [H] Condition is required and blocks like flaws (A-18, DD-04). The bin is a typed field that resellers will use (D-11).
    • [H] The 3 free listings are the first 3 items drafted; the rest of the haul is saved and locked until a plan is chosen (D-1). The sample haul has 5 items so this shows.
    • [H] Agreed by the PM (A-19): a trial seller who drafts exactly 3 items sees the plans at Finish haul, without anything being blocked (DD-08). Turn on "Haul has exactly 3 items" under Simulate.
    • [H] Decided (A-20): Undo has a 10-second countdown (decided 2026-11-14) that pauses while it's touched or focused; Profile → Accessibility → Keep Undo until I dismiss it turns the timer off (WCAG 2.2.1, DD-14).
    • [H] Onboarding asks for marketplaces, an account, then camera and microphone, before the first haul (R-33). Maya's persona argues for setup after the first listing (PQ-14). Turn on Onboarding: photo first under Test variants to compare (DD-41).
    • [H] Home's order and whether Profit stays a tab are decided by the prototype test. Both variants are under Simulate → Test variants (Q-09, Q-14).
    • [H] Sellers save photos to their library to post them (D-3). The album is "Listly – {item name}"; with add-only or limited access, photos go to Recents (DD-10).
    • [H] A three-way shipping choice, preselected from Selling defaults, is quick enough that sellers rarely skip it. Skipped shipping is an estimate, never $0 (D-9).
    • [H] Sellers tick "I removed it" on each marketplace reliably enough to keep the ledger true. Listly never says a listing is down until the seller confirms (R-15).
    • [H] Clear-out Pass sellers get the checklist and Needs action (D-2), but no ledger, stale filter, Profit tab or cost (R-18, PQ-08).
    • [H] Agreed for now (G-09, G-10): trial sellers see the Reseller screens; an expired Pass keeps copy, Mark sold and the checklist, and only drafting is locked (DD-26, DD-27).
    • [H] The Phase 1 price field is the seller's own price. The estimate (R-11) is a later release and is always labelled a guess (G-17, DD-12).
    • [H] Connections: Poshmark, Mercari, Depop and Facebook Marketplace can only be linked by username. The eBay connection depends on eBay's current API terms (R-34, R-35). Listly never asks for a marketplace password.
    • [H] First-run tips (up to 3 per screen) help rather than get dismissed; the 2-minute how-to video and steps are enough help for MVP (R-36, R-37).
    • [H] Marketplace fee rates and title limits are placeholders (Q-04). "Money made" for the Pass is sale prices before fees (G-11).
    • [H] Design-system gaps: GO has only GoButton and GoCard. Every other part is a gap styled from GO tokens and labelled — turn on Component labels. GO has no error, warning or helper-text colour, so status is shown with words, weight and borders, never colour alone. Full gap list: design-system-readiness.md. Platform UI (permission prompts, the lock screen) is labelled System /.
    • [H] Light theme only. GO's dark theme isn't applied here (PQ-16).
