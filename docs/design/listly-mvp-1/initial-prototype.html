    Assumptions to validate

    What this prototype assumes. Each [H] item is a hypothesis to confirm with the PM, SME or engineering.

    • [H] Photos from one haul can be grouped into items automatically, accurately enough that checking the grouping is quicker than tagging each item by hand. A Next item tap while shooting is the fallback.
    • [H] Resellers will answer brand, size, measurements and flaws by voice, and voice capture works in a normal home. Typing is the fallback.
    • [H] Collecting facts for every item first, then drafting all of them at once, beats the item-by-item flow the marketplaces use.
    • [H] The 3 free listings are full quality, and the paywall appears at Finish haul, when the value is on screen. "Not now" keeps those 3 listings copyable. Whether a 7-day trial that renews automatically converts better is untested.
    • [H] Onboarding asks for marketplaces, then an account, then camera and microphone, before the first haul. Creating the account before the first photo (not after the first draft) is a guess to test.
    • [H] Home's order (Needs action, unfinished haul, this month, stale items, plan) suits resellers. Sections with nothing in them are hidden.
    • [H] Listings shows one row per item per marketplace by default, with a By item view for the per-item ledger. Clear-out Pass users see only the Active filter.
    • [H] Connections: Poshmark, Mercari, Depop and Facebook Marketplace can only be linked by username, because they don't let apps post, remove or read listings. Linking lets Listly open the seller's listings and match forwarded sale emails.
    • [H] eBay connection through eBay's own sign-in, reading orders so eBay sales appear on their own, is allowed under eBay's current API terms. Its 2026 license update adds restricted APIs and AI-content rules, so this needs checking before it's built.
    • [H] Listly never asks for or stores a marketplace password.
    • [H] First-run tips appear once, on the first visit to Home, Copy listings and Listings, with 1–3 tips each, and can be skipped. Whether tips help, or get dismissed unread, needs testing.
    • [H] A 2-minute how-to video with captions and a transcript, plus written steps, is enough help for MVP. Who makes the video, and its length, are undecided. The Home card shows until it's dismissed.
    • [H] Showing both plans side by side, with the Reseller plan marked for regular sellers, doesn't confuse occasional sellers.
    • [H] Sellers will paste listing links back into Listly, and tick "I removed it" on each marketplace, reliably enough to keep the ledger true without marketplace APIs.
    • [H] The sold-everywhere checklist is enough without automatic delisting. Listly never says a listing is down until the seller confirms.
    • [H] Marketplace fee rates (Poshmark 20%, eBay 13.25%, Mercari 10%) and title character limits are placeholders. They must be verified and kept current before profit figures are shown.
    • [H] A price estimate labeled "A guess, not based on sold prices" is useful rather than misleading.
    • [H] The Clear-out Pass hides the ledger, checklist and profit tracker, and an upgrade nudge after 10+ items or a second marketplace is welcome, not naggy.
    • [H] The bin labels, the sale-email forwarding and the "fix photo" step are deferred to the next release, so they appear only as text here.
    • [H] Design-system gaps: GO Mobile Design System has only GoButton (no secondary variant) and GoCard. Every other part here is a gap styled from GO tokens: secondary and text buttons, tabs, segmented control, chips and tags, list rows, text fields, checkbox, tab bar, progress bar, toast, plan card, mic and shutter buttons, inline error and alarm banner, progress dots, settings groups, switches, sort control, a destructive button, coach-mark tips, video player and accordion. GO also has no error or warning colour role and no helper-text colour; this prototype uses borders, weight and the border colour instead. Turn on Component labels to see which is which.
    • [H] This prototype shows the light theme only. GO's dark theme isn't applied here.
